Core Architecture

Built directly on the institution, not around it

Generic consumer wallets force students to download an external payment app with full access to outside merchants. TrustID operates strictly within the verified college perimeter.

Issuance

One Mandatory Credential

Issued at matriculation alongside academic enrollment. It serves as both official campus identification and institutional payment mechanism.

Controls

Parent-Governed Rules

Parents set spending ceilings across categories like cafeterias, bookstores, and stationery, preventing allowance dissipation on unverified vendors.

Security

Strict Allow-List Ledger

Balances exist purely within TrustID's internal ledger. Funds cannot be swept out to open UPI networks or transferred peer-to-peer to outside accounts.

System Workflow

How the closed loop functions

From initial enrollment to everyday merchant checkout, funds remain accountable from deposit to redemption.

PHASE 01

Campus Issue

The institution provisions the student credential with a linked internal wallet upon enrollment.

PHASE 02

Parent Funding

Parents top up funds securely online and configure categorical daily or weekly velocity limits.

PHASE 03

Point of Sale

Students present their ID at verified university stalls, libraries, and canteens for instant ledger debit.

PHASE 04

Real-Time Sync

Every checkout is logged to the ledger, notifying parents instantly with exact merchant and category details.
