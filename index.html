Closed-Loop Architecture

Engineered for accountability inside the institution

Generic consumer wallets invite uncontrolled spending and security blind spots. TrustID keeps every transaction accountable within an isolated campus ledger.

Issuance

One Mandatory Credential

Issued at college enrollment alongside existing academic identity records. Identifies the student and powers verified point-of-sale checkouts.

Controls

Parent Guard Circuit

Parents set velocity limits and category restrictions (cafeterias, bookstores, uniform stalls) with instant card-freeze capability.

Security

Allow-Listed Merchants

No funds can leave the system to external UPI IDs. Spends execute purely against verified campus merchants via signed internal tokens.

The Commerce Loop

How TrustID functions across campus

A continuous chain of verification from initial deposit to end-of-day concession settlement.

PHASE 01

College Provisioning

Students receive their credential linked to an isolated ledger wallet at enrollment.

PHASE 02

Parent Top-Up

Parents fund allowances and set daily limits directly via card or bank gateway.

PHASE 03

Dynamic QR Spend

Students present short-lived, signed dynamic tokens at campus stalls for instant debit.

PHASE 04

Live Audit Stream

Every transaction logs to the append-only ledger and notifies parents with merchant details.
